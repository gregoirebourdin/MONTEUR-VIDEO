EN: Your Reel worked. The comments poured in, and Manychat sent every link. Then the replies started. "How much is it?" "Is this for me?" "Can we talk?" And nobody answered.

## 2. AMPLIFY — ce que ça coûte (~9 s)
EN: Because you were busy. On a call. Or asleep. By the time you reply, they've moved on. Your content brought them in… and your inbox let them go.
FR: Parce que tu étais occupé. En call. Ou en train de dormir. Le temps que tu répondes, ils sont passés à autre chose. Ton contenu les a fait venir… et ta boîte de réception les a laissés partir.

EN: Meet ManySetter. The AI setter that answers your Manychat leads. Instantly. In your voice.

## 4. HOW — comment ça marche, 3 temps (~13 s)
EN: It learns your offer from your own pages. It answers questions and objections like you would. And when a lead is a fit, it books the call. Right in the DM.

EN: So instead of a pile of unread DMs, you wake up to booked calls. It never sleeps, never ghosts a lead, and never asks for a commission. And you can take over anytime.

FR: Crée et teste ton setter gratuitement. Sans carte bancaire. Tu vends une offre à 1 500 $ ? Un seul client en plus paie toute l'année.
