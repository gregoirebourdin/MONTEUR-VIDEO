## Intent

A series of vertical reels for Instagram and YouTube Shorts, led by Setty, ManySetter's new mascot:
a living DM bubble, "super jolie, ultra mignonne, ultra catchy", with an attitude (insolente).
The edit must be "ultra dynamique, plein de motion", never boring for a second, and never cheap:
"comme si c'était édité par un bête de monteur". The hook lands in the first second.

## Assets

- assets/setty/setty.js — Setty, built procedurally in Three.js (pose and expression API).
- assets/fonts/ — Nohemi (brand display) and Inter, as in the ManySetter promo.
- ../manysetter-ad/film2/frame.md — brand truth: palette, type, app components, motion curves.
- ../manysetter-ad/research/, ../manysetter-ad/assets_in/ — app captures and CSS for real UI.

## Customizations

- No Veo and no paid generation: the mascot, its animation, the motion design, the music and the
  SFX are all made in code here. The voice is Charon (a few cents per reel).
- Setty speaks with Charon's voice: the contrast between a cute bubble and a deep, confident voice is
  part of the joke.
- Every spoken word on screen as kinetic type; an SFX on every motion; real app UI only.
- Deliver each reel with the mix, and a version without music for trending sounds added in-app.

## Notes

- Safe zones: keep text and key action out of the bottom ~25% and right edge (IG/YT buttons).
