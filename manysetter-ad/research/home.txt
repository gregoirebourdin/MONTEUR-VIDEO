FAQ
Questions before you start
Does ManySetter replace Manychat?
No. It works with the Manychat flows that already start your conversations.
How is it different from a chatbot?
It runs on its own setting model, learns your offer and voice, and books a call only when your fit rule is met. See
how the setter works
.
Will it sound like my brand?
Yes. You set its tone and words, record your voice for audio follow-ups, and test every answer before going live.
Can a person take over a conversation?
Yes. Choose when a conversation moves to a person, so your team steps in when judgment matters.
Can it book the call itself?
Yes, with Cal.com, iClosed or Calendly on a paid plan: it offers open times in the lead’s time zone, confirms their email and books. Otherwise, it sends your link.
Will it book more calls automatically?
No tool can promise a number of calls. ManySetter qualifies leads consistently and gives them a clearer path to book. Results depend on your leads, offer and flow.
Do I need to rebuild my flows?
No. Start with a flow that already brings in leads and connect the setter where the conversation needs to continue.
