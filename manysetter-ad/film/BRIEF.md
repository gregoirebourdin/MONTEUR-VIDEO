## Intent

The hero film for the ManySetter website (16:9 first; a dedicated 9:16 ad cut comes after).
The client wants a film that looks like a world-class motion designer made it, entirely in the
app's own design system. Calm, premium confidence: no hype, no invented numbers.
Full production bible: `../brief/PROMPT.md` (French) — scripts, shot list, tokens, motion
language, sound plan, QA.

- UI rebuilt as vector HTML from the real app (read-only capture of the "ManySetter demos" workspace): Inbox (AI summary, Why this reply, Reply myself), Playbook (Built from 6 documents), Booking & follow-ups, Hand to a person. All lead data fictional and in English.
